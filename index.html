One app. A few useful things.

01

Conversations with your people.

Private rooms and group chats, with history kept on your device. Share an invitation to bring someone in.

02

Music, video, and public ideas.

Share a file or a post. A public link helps people find it; peers supply the media directly.

03

A network you take part in.

Choose your storage and bandwidth budget. Keep the people you follow close, and contribute spare room for public posts.

What lives where

Small hubs.
Real connections.

A hub helps peers find each other and indexes public metadata and text. It does not keep your private chat history or host the music and video files.

Media and history come from an online peer. When a direct connection is unavailable, a relay can carry small live messages. If nobody with an authorized copy is online, the history or file has to wait.

This is an early beta. We’re working toward a dependable everyday app, and we’ll be clear about what works along the way.
