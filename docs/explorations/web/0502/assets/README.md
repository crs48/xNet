## Shared table — `shared-table.png`

Exact prompt:

> Create a refined editorial illustration for a research website titled From attention to shared agency. No lettering anywhere. Wide landscape 3:2 composition. A quiet sculptural miniature of a communal round worktable, seen from a slightly elevated angle, made from warm white folded paper and pale stone. Several distinct winding charcoal and muted cobalt ribbons approach the table from different directions; at the table they form an open braided structure, with some threads staying separate, then continue outward into a few small planted gardens and simple unfinished architectural models. Suggest people making common ground without erasing differences. Tactile paper grain, subtle natural shadows, precise art direction, restrained charcoal, slate blue, ivory and tiny ochre accents. Gallery-quality concept art, spacious, humane, contemplative but alive. Not a network graph, not futuristic technology, no glowing nodes, no logos, no identifiable real people. This is a conceptual illustration, not a product screenshot.

## Gift shelf — `gift-shelf.png`

Exact prompt:

> Create a sophisticated editorial illustration for a research essay about how small social structures enable cooperation. No lettering or readable text. Wide landscape 3:2 composition. A modest open communal shelf in a sunlit shared courtyard, with a few useful objects: a folded blanket, two ceramic bowls, a plant cutting in a glass jar, a small hand tool, books, and a basket. Two anonymous cropped human hands, one placing the plant on the shelf, another taking a bowl; no faces. The shelf and courtyard are a tactile miniature constructed of ivory paper, pale wood, softly textured plaster, charcoal outlines and muted cobalt accents with a little warm ochre. Restrained, beautiful, warm, human, precise shadows, plenty of visual breathing room. The emotional idea is that making a place to offer and receive makes cooperation possible. Not charity advertising, no poverty stereotypes, no app UI, no logos. Match the palette of an understated contemporary architectural model.
