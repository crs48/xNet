How hubs work

A hub keeps your devices connected

Local-first doesn’t mean living on an island. A hub can relay changes and keep backups while your devices come and go. Core editing stays on your device.

You can run the open-source hub yourself or choose managed hosting. Those choices have different trust boundaries: a managed operator runs the software that stores and indexes your content. Hosting is a service you choose, with responsibilities worth understanding.
