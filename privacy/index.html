Where your data lives.

This describes the current desktop beta, not the retired workspace or SDK.

Your desktop profile contains your identity keys, local messages, preferences, and cached files. Keep it private and back it up carefully. The new app does not read or convert an old Electron profile.

Public posts and media metadata are announced to the tracker. Public content is public: another person can retain a copy. Peer routing also reveals network addresses to the peers and infrastructure involved. This is not an anonymity network.

The hub does not store private chat history or media files. Small live messages can pass through the relay. History and media require an authorized peer. Share private invitation links only with the intended people; they contain access credentials.

Storage controls limit what this device retains. They cannot erase a copy already held by somebody else. Blocking, moderation, and revocation do not promise remote deletion.

The current beta has important limits, including no account recovery and no forward-secret group ratchet. Review the beta limits before using it for sensitive communication.

This site serves its own artwork and fonts. The migration does not enable a third-party analytics service. Report a security issue privately.
