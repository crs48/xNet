Sub-processors

Who helps us run xNet Cloud, what they receive, and where they operate.

Last updated: June 24, 2026

These third parties process data on our behalf to provide xNet Cloud, our managed, paid service. The local-first app and self-hosted hubs use none of them. Services you choose to connect yourself (such as GitHub, Notion, Slack, or your own webhooks) are your integrations, not our sub-processors, and their own terms govern.

Provider Purpose Data processed Location
WorkOS Accounts, authentication, SSO/SCIM Email, name, account identifier United States
Stripe Payments and AI usage metering Email, customer reference, plan, charge amounts United States
Google Cloud (Cloud Run, Firestore) Hub compute and control-plane state Tenant metadata, AI usage totals, your running hub Per plan; region-pinned for Enterprise
Cloudflare R2 Encrypted content blobs and database backups Encrypted tenant content, database snapshots Cloudflare network
OpenRouter Managed AI gateway (opt-in feature) Prompt and message content you send to the AI United States / selected model provider
Plausible Analytics Cookieless website analytics Page views, country, device type (no personal data) European Union

Changes to this list

We update this page when we add, remove, or change a sub-processor and bump the date above. Business customers under our Data Processing Addendum receive advance notice of new sub-processors and may object as described there. For questions, contact privacy@xnet.fyi.
