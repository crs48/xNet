Roadmap

The current alpha, the next practical improvements, and the longer-term goals.

Product snapshot: October 2026 · These are priorities, not delivery dates.

Available now · Alpha

Workspace and developer foundations

The desktop and browser workspace already bring documents, databases, and canvases together. The desktop library collects saved sources. Underneath are local storage, signed changes, sync, and an open protocol.

  • · Desktop releases and a browser demo
  • · Documents, databases, canvases, and a desktop library
  • · Published developer packages and self-hostable hub software
Explore what you can use →
Current focus

Everyday reliability and AI review

The next measure is ordinary use: can someone write, plan, collect, and organise here day after day? Reliability, recovery, and the small points of friction matter as much as new features.

  • · Keep improving startup, recovery, and everyday editing
  • · Make agent work useful and its effects easy to review
  • · Move from technical setup toward an integrated experience
Follow what is actually shipping →
Next horizon

Simpler hosting and recovery

Hub and hosting foundations exist. The goal is to make a private, durable, connected workspace straightforward for someone who doesn’t want to operate a server.

  • · Simpler onboarding and cross-device setup
  • · Clear recovery and portable identity
  • · Hosted convenience alongside a real self-hosting option
Understand the hosting direction →
Longer-term vision

Shared tools and knowledge

Build a place where people can discover, share, and adapt what others make. Publishing and identity work provide some foundations; the broader discovery and circulation experience remains a direction, not a finished network.

  • · Useful tools and knowledge shared with provenance
  • · A commons that grows from real communities
  • · Connections to the wider decentralised web
