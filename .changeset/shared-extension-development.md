Add optional development-record fields to chat messages so applications can index
shared extension projects and retain attributed requests and version history within
the conversation's existing authorization boundary.

Community dependency rooms deliver a top-level circle and its creator-issued membership records to invited collaborators. They keep normal node-change envelopes and require membership on authenticated hosts. Sparse message edits and redactions now reach channel recipients even when the update omits its schema and channel.
